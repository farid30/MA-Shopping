===================================================================
MA Shopping - Android Application Package (APK) Installation Guide
===================================================================

This folder contains the pre-compiled Android APK ready for installation on any Android phone or tablet:

1. Files included:
   - apk/app-debug.apk (Direct installable debug APK package)
   - apk/MA-Shopping-v1.0.apk (Identical copy with versioned filename)
   - ./app-debug.apk (Root-level copy for convenience)

2. How to install on your Android device:
   Step 1: Transfer 'app-debug.apk' to your Android device via USB cable, Google Drive, WhatsApp, Telegram, or direct download.
   Step 2: On your phone, tap the downloaded APK file.
   Step 3: If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap Settings and toggle ON "Allow from this source".
   Step 4: Tap "Install" to complete the installation.
   Step 5: Open "MA Shopping" from your home screen or app drawer!

3. Generating a fresh APK anytime in Google AI Studio:
   - Click the top right settings / export menu in Google AI Studio.
   - Choose "Export Project" (download full ZIP containing this apk folder)
     or "Generate APK / AAB".
   - Or in terminal run: gradle assembleDebug
